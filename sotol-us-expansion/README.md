# Sotol Hacienda: Strategic Roadmap to US On-Premise Expansion

**1st place of 8 teams** in Columbia APAN 5800's final case competition (Fall 2025, team project).

> The underlying sales data is confidential and is **not** included in this repository. This page summarizes the research approach, findings and recommendations only.

## The business problem

Sotol Hacienda is a premium, USDA Organic sotol, a spirit distilled from the desert-grown Dasylirion plant. It entered the US through Texas in 2023 and expanded to California soon after. Early traction was strong in premium venues but inconsistent in general-market venues.

**Question:** Which US city should become Sotol's next growth hub, and which venue types should it target?

## Approach

1. **Data:** distributor on-premise sales for Texas and California (monthly, Jan–Oct 2024 vs. Jan–Oct 2025), plus on-premise alcohol market data for New York and other major cities.
2. **Data cleaning:** standardized about 390 venues by fixing inconsistent service-type and location labels and aligning data types across all variables.
3. **Segmentation:** grouped venues by service type (bar, dining, hotel), sub-segment (e.g., fine dining, luxury, cocktail, casual), cuisine, city and region.
4. **Metrics:** calculated average monthly sales, reorder frequency, sales volatility, year-over-year growth and a venue performance index.
5. **Trend analysis:** compared month-over-month patterns to find segments with both high value and high retention.

## The Tableau story (7 story points)

1. Market footprint: Texas and California heat maps
2. Today's on-premise landscape: market segmentation
3. 2024 vs. 2025 sub-segment performance
4. Cross-metric segment comparison: operational consistency
5. Segment value and retention analysis
6. NYC spirits market trends: premium spirit and tequila growth
7. NYC opportunity areas: maps of restaurants and hotels

## Key findings

- Sotol's US footprint was fragmented, with no single anchor market.
- Most of Sotol's volume came from premium sub-segments (fine dining, luxury, premium casual, cocktail bars), where consumers already seek craft spirits.
- Many general-market venues did not turn Sotol's premium positioning into repeat orders.

## Recommendations

1. **Prioritize premium venues in NYC:** increase investment in fine dining and luxury hotels, where demand is strongest.
2. **Target Midtown and Lower Manhattan first:** focus sales, activations and partnerships where premium venues cluster.
3. **Optimize spend by exiting low-yield segments:** shift budget away from general-market venues with weak reorders.
4. **Position Sotol as the premium agave alternative:** sell it as a tequila trade-up in NYC's highest-value venues.
